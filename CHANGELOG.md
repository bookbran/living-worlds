# Changelog

What each version changed, newest first. The Astrolabe store shows these notes when it offers an update.

## 0.2.1

Walk through any world with a game controller, and turn the Maps gallery off or on from any chat.

## 0.2.0

A season switch turns the leaves for fall, the view turns like Google Earth with a compass, cars come in their own sizes, and a world can carry porches, gates, gardens, storefronts, steeples and tile roofs.

## 0.1.0

First release on its own. Living worlds started inside Groundstation and now installs separately, with its own button and the Maps gallery.
