__TITLE__, a living world

scroll to fly toward the pointer · drag to look around (the view follows the mouse) · right-drag to move the way the mouse goes (up and down to rise and sink, sideways to slide) · Shift or Ctrl + drag, or middle-drag, to orbit the spot under the pointer · the compass turns, faces north and tilts · W A S D to glide, Q E down and up, Shift for speed
